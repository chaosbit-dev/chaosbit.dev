Gonzo · Discord bot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Gonzo is a self-hosted Discord bot that manages a private Minecraft server for a small, invite-only community. It is a personal/hobby project, not a commercial service. This policy explains exactly what data the bot touches — the short version is: very little, and none of it is sold, shared, or used for anything but running the bot.

What Gonzo processes

Discord user IDs. The bot keeps a small allowlist mapping specific Discord user IDs to a role (OP or controller) so it can decide who may run privileged commands. This list lives in the operator's private configuration and is used only for authorization.
Slash-command inputs. When you run a command (e.g. /status, or later /mod request), the text you submit is processed transiently to carry out that command. Command inputs may appear in short-lived operational logs for debugging.
Minecraft server status. The bot reads the server's health from its own infrastructure (not from you) to answer status commands.

What Gonzo does NOT do

  • Does not read your message content — it runs on slash commands only, with the Discord "Message Content" intent disabled.
  • Does not store message history, build a profile, or track your activity.
  • Does not collect member lists, presence, email, payment, or location data.
  • Does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party trackers, and never sells or rents any data.

Where it runs & who it talks to

Gonzo runs on the operator's self-hosted home infrastructure. It communicates with Discord's API to function. Certain features contact additional services without sending your personal data: opening plugin-request pull requests via GitHub, and validating plugin metadata via Modrinth / SpigotMC. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Retention

The role allowlist persists in the operator's private configuration until changed. Operational logs are transient and rotate away; there is no user database. Removing the bot from a server, or asking the operator to remove your ID, ends any processing of your data.

Children

Gonzo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your choices

You can stop all data processing by not using the bot's commands, leaving the server, or contacting the operator to have your ID removed from the allowlist.
