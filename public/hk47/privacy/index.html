HK-47 · Discord bot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

HK-47 is a self-hosted Discord bot that plays a character from Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic in a small, invite-only family server. It is a personal/hobby project, not a commercial service.

Read this part. Unlike most bots of this size, HK-47 reads the text of messages in the channels it is configured for, and sends that text to a large language model in order to write a reply. Depending on availability, that model runs either on the operator's own hardware or on Anthropic's API — meaning your message text can leave the operator's network. If you would rather that not happen, do not type in the channels HK-47 is active in.

What HK-47 processes

  • Message content. In allowlisted channels only. Your message is used to generate one reply and is not stored afterwards. It may appear briefly in operational logs for debugging.
  • Discord user IDs and display names. Stored on the operator's own disk. The ID is used to recognise you across the game server and Discord, and as a key for reply rate-limits and mention throttling; the display name is cached so the bot can refer to you by name.
  • A short description of known players. The operator writes a one-line, in-character opinion for each known person (this is the joke), plus factual relationships such as "sibling of". These are held in the bot's configuration and included in the text sent to the model.
  • Game server activity. Join and leave events from a private Palworld server, used to announce them in character.

Where your message text goes

To generate a reply, the bot sends the message text, the speaker's display name, and the player descriptions above to a language model. It tries a model running on the operator's own hardware first. If that is unavailable it falls back to Anthropic's API, which is a third party with its own privacy policy. No other third parties receive your data.

What HK-47 does NOT do

  • Does not retain message content after replying, or build a searchable history of what you said.
  • Does not read channels it has not been explicitly configured for, or direct messages.
  • Does not collect member lists, presence, email, payment, or location data.
  • Does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party trackers, and never sells or rents any data.
  • Does not train any model on your messages. They are sent for inference only.

Where it runs

HK-47 runs on the operator's self-hosted home infrastructure and communicates with Discord's API to function. Stored data (IDs, cached display names) lives on that hardware and is not backed up to any third party.

Retention

Message text: not retained. Discord IDs and cached display names: kept for as long as the bot is configured to know you, and removable on request. Operational logs: short-lived, and rotated out by the host.

Your choices

You can stop all data processing by not typing in the channels HK-47 is active in, leaving the server, or contacting the operator to have your ID and description removed from its configuration.

Children

The bot's in-character output is written for an adult audience and is not directed at children.
